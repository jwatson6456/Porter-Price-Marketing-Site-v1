Based on the latest earnings, we estimate that Nvidia is worth $326.50 per share. A disciplined investor doesn’t pay full price — you want a margin of safety of 20% to protect against being wrong. That makes the fair price to pay — what we call the Porter Price — $261.20. With shares currently trading at $223.36, that points to a potential upside of 16.9%.
This is not financial advice. The Porter Price is one analytical estimate, not a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Figures change with the market and with each earnings report. Do your own research and consider your own circumstances before investing.

What is the Porter Price? It’s our estimate of the highest price a rational investor should pay for a business today — its intrinsic value, discounted by a margin of safety so you’re protected if the future doesn’t go to plan. Who Is Porter

25 years calling the market straight

In 1999, Porter Stansberry started a financial-research firm from a Baltimore apartment. It grew into one of the largest independent research businesses in the world. He walked away from it to build something with no conflicts and no Wall Street masters — Porter & Co., run today from a barn on his Maryland farm.

The mission is simple: help serious, self-directed investors own and understand exceptional businesses, think independently, and reduce uncertainty in their investing.
